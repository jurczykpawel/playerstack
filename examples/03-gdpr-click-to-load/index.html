Click-to-load: zero third-party cookies until interaction

Open DevTools → Network → Cookies before clicking the player. You'll see no requests to youtube.com or googlevideo.com at all. Click play — that's when the YouTube iframe actually loads.

Why this matters for GDPR / Privacy Sandbox:
  • YouTube embeds normally drop tracking cookies on page load — even before play
  • EU regulators have ruled this requires consent (multiple GDPR fines)
  • With clickToLoad: true, the iframe never loads until interaction
  • No consent banner needed for the embed itself
  • ~200 KB of YouTube JS deferred → faster Largest Contentful Paint

Use Privacy Badger or your browser's tracker counter to verify. Refresh, count trackers before clicking, then click and count again.
